Which situation are we planning for?
Say on every call: "Stage 4–5 dementia, currently with acute delirium (may be from an infection). He does not sleep. Looking for a stay of up to 3 months starting around Friday, October 30." Ask: Is a bed open? Is the room inside the locked unit? Can we bring in our own private PSW? Is a nurse on site overnight? What paperwork before admission (doctor's form, TB test, x-ray)?
